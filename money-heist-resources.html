Key Takeaways

Five ideas to remember after exploring the Money Heist strategy lessons.

  • Preparation reduces uncertainty before execution.
  • Strong plans include alternatives when circumstances change.
  • Teams perform better when individual strengths are aligned with suitable roles.
  • Emotional discipline can help maintain clarity during difficult decisions.
  • Communication and storytelling can influence how people understand an idea.
