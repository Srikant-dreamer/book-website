What Is This?

A bridge between physical reading and digital learning.

A physical book gives you the core reading experience. This digital library adds another layer to it.

Readers can scan a QR code inside a physical book and enter a digital space containing additional videos, audio, resources, quizzes, references and interactive learning material.

The goal is not to replace physical books. It is to make them more connected, interactive and useful.
